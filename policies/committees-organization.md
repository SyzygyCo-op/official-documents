# Syzygy Committees Organization

This document is to describe the establishment and implementation of committees, committee chairs, and the executive council at Syzygy SF. This document is presented as a proposal, to be discussed with the members of Syzygy before coming to a final agreement.

## Background

In past years, Syzygy SF was run in a very ad-hoc way, based on what people were excited about and when people had free time. As we grow, we have established a more structured, more scalable, long term approach based on the following objectives:

- Clarifies division and organization of responsibilities and initiatives
- Distributes responsibilities and initiatives more evenly
- Enables members to contribute in more ways
- Enables members to contribute in ways that they enjoy more
- Empowers members to create the changes they want to see at Syz
- Empowers members to delegate tasks to one another
- Enables members to pass on responsibilities to others and take breaks.

## High Level

At the high level, we would like to set up committees within Syzygy. Every committee would be led by a committee chair and made up of Syzygy members. The committee chairs, along with the President, would make up the Executive Council (EC). A member can only hold one role in the Executive Council. Every member must be in a committee, but members can choose which committee they want to be in.

## Committees

This section details each of the committees and their responsibilities. The committee chair is not responsible for doing all of these things. Instead, the committee chair is responsible for making sure that their committee is taking care of all their responsibilities by delegating and organizing the work across the members of their committee.

Furthermore, there will be many responsibilities and tasks that fall into a gray area between various committees. In that case, it is the responsibility of the committee chairs to figure out how those responsibilities or tasks will be split up between the committee chairs.

All Syzygy responsibilities must fall under some committee’s jurisdiction. If there are any responsibilities that no committee wants to take on, the committees must negotiate among themselves to figure out who has to take on that responsibility.

### Member Operations

### President

The president will not be in any of the committees. Their responsibilities are listed below:

- Coordinates between committees
  - Facilitates communications between committees.
  - Organizes projects, initiatives, and efforts across the committees so that they don’t interfere with one another or block one another.
  - Organizes regular meetings for the committee chairs
  - Makes sure that the committees are running smoothly.
- Identifies Goals / Visions
  - Identifies a high level vision for the organization in collaboration with the co-op and enables committees to execute that vision.
  - Facilitates goal-setting for the organization. Plans and executes a way toward those goals.
  - Promotes new ideas and innovation within Syzygy, even when these new ideas might not work out perfectly or when these ideas push us out of our comfort zones.
  - Pushes Syzygy to be as great as it can be and prevents Syzygy from stagnating or growing stale.
  - Keep Syzygy fresh and spontaneous.
  - Identifies high level problems and inefficiencies within Syzygy and works with committees to effectively remediate these issues.
  - Pushes Syzygy as an organization to continually grow from our past experiences.
- Executes Decisions as needed
  - Oversees work that falls outside of the committees’ jurisdictions
  - Executes decisions to take on situations, problems or goals as we face them.
- Inspires Syzygy members to do the most that they can with Syzygy

## Committees

Committees will be run by the committee chairs and made up of Syzygy members that decided to join that committee. Every Syzygy member will be required to join a committee upon joining. Syzygy members can switch to join a different committee at any time.

Committee meetings must be held at least monthly. Any Syzygy member can go to any committee meeting.

Besides this, each committee chair will decide how their committee is run. This includes things like:

- How decisions are made within the committee
- How committee meetings are run
- How work within the committee is delegated

Each committee will report on their affairs at each meeting. These reports can be done by a committee chair, by a member of the committee, or it can be provided asynchronously ahead of the meeting. This report will include any future plans, current projects, any decisions that they’ve made, any requests for help they might have and/or anything else they want to communicate to the rest of the organization.

Decisions that are made by a committee can be contested, taken to a discussion, or put to a vote by people outside of that committee. Committees should take input from people outside their committee. However, they are not obligated to do anything that anyone outside the committee requests, unless a vote was called to take a particular action. A majority vote by Syzygy members can always override any decision a committee makes.

## Executive Council

The executive council will consist of the president and the committee chairs. The executive council is expected to work together as a team to lead Syzygy and its members.

In cases of emergency, a quorum consisting of half or more members of the executive council will be enabled to take emergency actions without consulting the rest of the organization.

Besides this, the executive council is allowed no additional privileges.

## Nomination and Voting

The voting process will begin at the start of the new year in order to elect the members of the executive council. Committee chairs will start their term in February and it will last one year.

Nominations open at the first General Meeting of the year. Candidates for each position will be nominated anonymously by Syzygy members. Candidates need to be nominated twice in order to become candidates. After being nominated, members can choose to accept or reject their nomination. If no one is nominated, then we go through the same process as if we got a “no confidence”, which is to simply repeat the process.

After nomination, candidates will be given some time to put together a campaign platform and provide it to the organization. Member Operations will also organize a time for candidates to explain and describe their platforms, promote themselves, and answer questions. This will typically occur at the second General Meeting of the year, but can be extended if arranged by Member Operations committee ahead of time.

Voting will happen afterwards via a Google form. All the members will be given 2 weeks to vote. Members will be allowed to either vote for a candidate or vote no confidence.

The schedule for nomination and voting will be set by member operations and will be provided 2 weeks ahead of nominations at minimum.

If voting for a particular position results in no confidence, Member Operations will organize a new, accelerated nomination and vote cycle. This will repeat until all positions are filled.

## Executive Council Removal

The members have the ability to remove any member of the EC via a majority vote of all the members. If the vote to remove a member of the EC passes, that change becomes effective immediately.

Executive Council members can also choose to leave their position at any time.

If an EC member stops being part of the EC for any reason, Member Operations will organize a new, accelerated nomination and vote cycle. The EC will appoint an interim office-holder by plurality vote.

## Changing Committee Structure

It’s important to us to be able to be flexible with these committees, especially since we’re not sure how it’s going to go at the beginning.

As a result, we will allow all of the following to happen by majority vote to change this policy:

- Shifting responsibilities from one committee to another
- Renaming committees
- Splitting or merging committees

## Closed Discussion Topics

- “What if there’s a responsibility that no committee wants to take on?”
  - If it is a required responsibility, then the committees must negotiate amongst themselves to find out who will take that responsibility. This may involve:
    - Trading responsibilities
    - Receiving fewer responsibilities in exchange for taking on a particularly difficult responsibility
    - Receiving more fun responsibilities in exchange for taking on a particularly difficult responsibility.
- What do we think of doing 6 month terms instead of one year?
  - shorter terms can provide a lot more flexibility, especially since we don't know how this is going to go at first
  - however, elections are also work and doing more of them per year would be more work (but we can make elections less work)
  - People can appoint someone or step down as committee chair instead of needing to rely on built in term endings.
  - After some discussion, it did not seem like there was any significant, obvious or certain advantage to this. As a result, I think we should try out the 1 year term for now and revisit this issue if we want once we have more data and experience.
- Do we want to allow abstaining from the committee chair votes?
  - We currently allow abstaining in all other types of votes.
  - We should tackle this issue separately while proceeding with the Syzygy Committee Plan.
- We would like to restructure the split of responsibilities across committees.
  - Generally speaking, we would like to have more committees that each have less responsibility.
  - The Member Operations Committee looks like it covers a lot. Let’s split this up?
    - Maybe we can split it up into member operations vs member education or member communication?
  - Some people proposed an events committee.
    - Some people want it to be a group of volunteers dedicated to helping out with events
    - Other people want it to be an educational group where people who don’t know how to run events can learn.
    - Generally speaking, we don’t want an events committee that restricts what events people can do or what events you can volunteer for.
  - We are choosing to go with what we have right now and modify if we decide we need any changes soon.
